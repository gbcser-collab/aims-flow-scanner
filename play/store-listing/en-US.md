# AIMS Flow – Google Play listing (EN)

**App name:** AIMS Flow

**Short description:**  
Jobs, GPS tracking, CMR documents, driver alerts and dispatch messaging in one app.

**Full description:**  
AIMS Flow is the driver and transport operations app of Logistic-A.I.M.S.

It brings daily transport jobs, pickup and delivery stops, time windows, job documents, driver status reports and dispatch communication into one mobile workflow.

Key features:
• assigned jobs and stop overview
• pickup and delivery details, references and cargo information
• GPS-based trip and arrival tracking
• navigation to the next stop
• one-tap driver alerts for delay, waiting, technical issues and other problems
• direct messaging with dispatch
• capture and send CMR and other transport documents
• offline queues with automatic retry after network interruptions
• push notifications for new jobs and important operational events
• Hungarian, English and German interface

AIMS Flow is intended for business use. An authorized Logistic-A.I.M.S. driver or admin account is required.
